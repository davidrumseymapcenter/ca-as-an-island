# Origin of the idea of California 

---

* California had its beginnings as a mythical island in a popular work of romantic fiction.
* *Las Sergas de Esplandián (The Adventures of Esplandián)* was written in about 1510 by Spanish writer Garci Rodriguez de Mantalvo and published in Seville.
* In this book, California was inhabited only by women who were ruled by a black female Amazon warrior named Calafia. The women were protected by griffins, and their only metal was gold.
* Myths of legendary islands brimming with riches in a southern sea were known by Spanish explorers as early as the thirteenth-century explorations of Marco Polo.
* When Hernán Cortés explored Baja California in 1537, he thought he had landed on an island.
* Many historians believe that the name “Isla de California” was inspired by De Montalvo’s book, reflecting Spanish explorers’ ambitions, hopes, and dreams.
