## Copies Emerge

The very next year, prolific English cartographer John Speed copied this “new” image of California as an island on his map of the Americas. In contrast to Briggs, Speed’s map left the unexplored Pacific northwest open to interpretation. The map’s elaborate border includes plans of New World cities along the top and portrayals of indigenous peoples of the Americas along the sides.

## Inaccuracy is Repeated

In 1650, French royal cartographer Nicholas Sanson published his landmark map of North America. His map—the first to show all five of the great lakes and depicting California as an island—was widely circulated. The belief that California was an island had now been adopted and validated by some of the most respected cartographers in Europe. The map below is a 1669 copy selected for this set due to the ornate cartouche.
