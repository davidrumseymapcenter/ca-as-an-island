# Early maps of California 

---
 
* European cartographers actually already knew as early as the sixteenth century that California was not an island.
* Sixteenth century maps by European cartographers such as this map by Flemish cartographer Cornelis Wytfliet, show California as a peninsula.
* Spanish explorations of the Sea of Cortez that followed the Hernan Cortés expeditions also found California to be a peninsula.
